Born from a love of Naples and brought to Montreal, Fuoco has been serving rue Saint-Denis since 2019. Marco, our pizzaiolo trained in Naples, personally selects every ingredient: San Marzano tomatoes, Caputo flour, buffalo mozzarella delivered twice a week. Every pizza is hand-stretched in front of you, in an open kitchen, because transparency is part of the meal. Whether you dine in, take out, or order delivery, you get the same fire, the same flour, and the same family standards — every single time. Do you deliver to my neighbourhood?Livrez-vous dans mon quartier?

Yes! We deliver to the Plateau Mont-Royal, Mile End, Outremont and Centre-Sud. $2 delivery fee, free on orders of $40 and up.Oui ! Nous livrons dans le Plateau Mont-Royal, Mile End, Outremont et Centre-Sud. Frais de livraison de 2 $, gratuit pour toute commande de 40 $ et plus.

Do you have vegetarian or vegan options?Avez-vous des options végétariennes ou véganes?

Absolutely. Our dough is naturally vegan, and cashew-based vegan mozzarella is available on any pizza (+$3).Absolument. Notre pâte est naturellement végane, et notre mozzarella végane à base de cajou est disponible sur toutes les pizzas (+3 $).
